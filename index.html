Portfolio

A special projects studio
for democracy.

We're AI-first builders launching high-leverage initiatives for a more flourishing democracy.

We form small, fast-moving teams that take an idea, test it under live conditions, and—if it works—spin it out as an independent organization. We build the projects established organizations can't take on: the ones that are too risky, too novel, or too hard.

Get involved

We work across domains including

Consumer advocacy

Helping people make informed, values-aligned choices about the companies and products they support.

Community formation

Creating recurring local groups that combine social connection with civic action, from neighborhood meetups to volunteer clubs.

Public-service access

Helping people navigate government services and benefits more easily, including through free, multilingual support.

Civic participation

Making it easier for people to register to vote, find accurate election information, and understand what's on their ballot.

Worker empowerment

Giving workers better information about their jobs, their industries, and what they have in common with each other.

Organizing & mobilizing

Helping organizers and community groups reach, coordinate, and activate the people they serve.

Media & information

Encouraging the discovery and dissemination of fact-based information to the communities and people who need it.

Democratic accountability

Making information about public institutions and public figures easier to find, understand, and act on.

About Us

We are a fast-growing team of builders—founders, engineers, designers, product executives, journalists, marketers, and strategists—who have spent decades at the frontier of technology, media, and civic life.

We've founded companies later acquired by Microsoft, Google, Snap, and Zillow. We've shipped products at Amazon, Meta, Samsung, and beyond. We've reported from dozens of countries, built R&D hubs across Fortune 50 companies, and even led IPOs to the NYSE.
