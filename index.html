A special projects studio
for democracy.

We're AI-first builders taking time out of our regularly scheduled careers to launch high-leverage initiatives for a more flourishing democracy.

We form small, fast-moving teams that take an idea, test it under live conditions, and—if it works—spin it out as an independent organization. We build the projects established organizations can't take on: the ones that are too risky, too novel, or too hard.

We work across domains including

Democratic accountability

Building AI-enabled tools for investigating political candidates, monitoring threats to democratic institutions, and making complex patterns legible to the public.

Consumer advocacy

Helping people resist manipulation and shop their values by building a national consumer-advocacy network.

Worker empowerment

Revealing shared conditions across companies and sectors, giving workers better information about what they have in common.

Community formation

Creating recurring local groups that combine social connection with civic action—from book clubs that shelve books at libraries to running clubs that pick up trash.

Public-service access

Helping people navigate government services and benefits through a free, multilingual AI concierge available by phone or text.

Pro-democracy capacity

Giving civic organizations and skilled professionals the AI fluency, tools, and connections they need to do more effective work.

About Us

We are a fast-growing team of builders—founders, engineers, designers, product executives, journalists, marketers, and strategists—who have spent decades at the frontier of technology, media, and civic life.

We've founded companies later acquired by Microsoft, Google, Snap, and Zillow. We've shipped products at Amazon, Meta, Samsung, and beyond. We've reported from dozens of countries, built R&D hubs across Fortune 50 companies, and even led IPOs to the NYSE.
